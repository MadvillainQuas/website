Scouting

Epinoia is the public half: leagues, live scoring, tables anybody can read. Prophe(s)y Scouting is the working half — the analysis tools built on the same numbers, for the people who make decisions with them. It sits behind an invite, which is why this page exists: so there is something to read before there is something to sign into.

What is over there

leaderboards The full table Seventy-odd columns across every player in a competition, sortable and filterable, with percentile context rather than raw ranks alone.
players Profiles A player’s season in one page: shooting by zone, playmaking, on/off splits, who they play well beside, and how each reads against the league.
lineups With or without What a team does with a player on the floor and off it, across every combination that has actually played, with sample sizes shown rather than hidden.
scouting Cross-league translation Production moved between competitions of different standards, so a season in one league can be read against a season in another.

Access and safeguarding

Prophe(s)y Scouting is invite-only, and under-18 players are withheld from the public side entirely regardless of what an invited account can see. That is enforced by the database rather than by each page remembering.

Getting access

If you work in a club, a federation or an agency and the tools above would be useful, get in touch and say what you would use them for.
