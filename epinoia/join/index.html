Good to know

How do I cancel?
From Your profile: under Membership, press Cancel membership. It is online, one step, and you can do it at any time.
What does it cost?
The price on the plan, every month or every year, until you cancel. It includes any VAT. There is nothing else to pay.
The 14 days
Buying online normally gives you 14 days to change your mind. Membership starts the moment you pay, so when you ask for it to start straight away you give that right up. Cancelling at any time still stops the next payment.
Under 18
Memberships are bought by adults. A parent or guardian can buy one for a junior.
Who takes the payment?
Stripe, on its own secure page. Epinoia never sees or stores your card number.
Already a member?
Sign in with the email address your membership is under and it is recognised on every page straight away.
