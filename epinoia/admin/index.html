Whose competition this is

Men’s, women’s or mixed. Global scouting filters on it, so a recruiter can rank women’s leagues on their own rather than against men’s. Leave it unset and the league still appears — under “all”.

Merchandise

Epinoia builds a shirt, a hoodie, a scarf, a print and a mug from each club’s crest and colours, and shows them on the league’s front page. It takes no money and ships nothing — paste the address of the shop that does, and every item points at it. Leave it empty and the section says the shop is not open yet.

Photographs

Nothing is publicly visible until it is approved here. Approving moves the file into the public bucket; rejecting deletes it. A photograph of an under-18 player cannot be approved without recorded guardian consent — the database refuses it, whoever presses the button.

roles are granted by email address

A league-wide statistician role lets someone score any game in the league. To limit them to one fixture, assign them on that fixture instead.

Who can see this league

private — it opens only with a link

This league is not listed on Epinoia, is not searchable and is not in the sitemap. A link is the only way in. Send one to a group and everybody in it can read the fixtures, results and statistics; revoke it and it stops working, though the people who already used it stay in.
