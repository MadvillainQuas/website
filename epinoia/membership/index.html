Good to know

Cancelling, the price and who takes the payment

How do I cancel?
From Personalisation: under Membership, press Cancel membership. It is online, one step, and you can do it at any time.
What does it cost?
The price on the plan, every month or every year, until you cancel. It includes any VAT. There is nothing else to pay.
Who takes the payment?
Stripe, on its own secure page. Epinoia never sees or stores your card number.
Already a member?
Sign in with the email address your membership is under and it is recognised on every page straight away.
