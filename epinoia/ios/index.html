02

Game alerts

  1. Sign in

    Open EPINOIΛ, tap Sign in and enter your email. Type the code from the email, or open its link on the same iPhone.

  2. Follow your clubs and players

    On a club's or a player's page, tap Follow, then Turn on notifications.

  3. Allow notifications

    iOS asks the first time: choose Allow. Tip-off reminders, starting lineups, half-time and full-time scores then arrive on the lock screen.

    Chose Don't Allow? Open Settings › Notifications › EPINOIΛ and turn on Allow Notifications.

03

Before the app: your Home ScreenWithout the app

Add EPINOIΛ to your Home Screen from Safari: it opens full screen, like an app.

  1. Open Epinoia in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open EPINOIΛ from the new icon and turn notifications on from your Profile page. Game alerts need iOS 16.4 or later.

If you use both, turn notifications off in the Home Screen copy (its Profile page), so alerts do not arrive twice.
