Sign in first

The clock cam sends the hall's clock to the game's broadcast graphics, so it needs an account that may broadcast the game: a league admin, a manager of either club, or the assigned statistician.

Which game?

Games on today, or about to be. Tap one.

Drag a rectangle tightly round the clock digits (include the colon). Keep the phone still: a clamp or a rail beats a hand. Digits should be level and at least the height of your thumbnail on screen.

PC mode posts the boxed clock to the league's PC a few times a second; on the PC run python scripts\worker\clock_cam.py --game <id> --source phone. Use it when the phone's own reading keeps missing (a dim or unusual board).
Readings are held until they run at the speed of time or repeat; a reading that jumps the clock forward is ignored unless it repeats. The graphics tick between readings and slide to each correction, so a missed frame never shows.
