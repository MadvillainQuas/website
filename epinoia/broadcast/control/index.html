Tap start on the throw-in and stop on the whistle (or press Space). While you keep it, every layer runs from your taps; the feed only nudges the time when it disagrees by more than a second and a half. To read the hall's board with a camera instead, open the clock cam on a phone or run the PC reader (see the tutorial).
