# The public section (/epinoia/) is meant to be found, slowly. The pages are static, but most of them
# fill themselves from a small database when they are opened, and a crawler that renders a page opens
# it: a fast crawl is a load on the site's database, not on its host. So: come, read, and take your time.
#
# CRAWL-DELAY is seconds between requests. Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yandex, Baidu and most others honour it
# (10 s is about 8,000 pages a day, far more than this site has). Google does not read it and sets its
# own pace, backing off when a site answers 429 or 503.

User-agent: *
Crawl-delay: 10
# (everything not named below is open: that is the default, so there is no Allow line to outweigh the rules)
